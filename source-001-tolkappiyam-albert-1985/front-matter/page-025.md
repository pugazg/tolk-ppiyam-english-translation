This very effort at the ordering brings in additional areas of insights and interests. This is true of any grammar of the proportion of Tolkāppiyam. But what adds to the tremendous appreciation one has for this work is its historical perspective.

In the centuries before Christ if one were to compose a grammar of this sort one has to become aware of a huge amount of past literature and its presence in one’s self at a moment of actuality as reflected in the actual speaker’s in their loving and reverential usage of their language in speech and in writing. The consciousness a linguist requires and the amount of reflection on what a whole community does as internalized in himself analysing the corpus of a language with all its remifications in those centuries compel us to see what work of astonishing effort Tolkāppiyam is.

This sort of historical approach to the appreciation of Tolkāppiyam is a common place among Tamil scholars. But there is another historical perspective that comes altogether as a discovery to this translator.

Confining ourselves to the two parts Phonology and Morphology we can say that the approach and concepts in Tolkāppiyam come very near those of the most recent Higher Grammars in English that are the results of a hundred years of Linguistics studies in Europe. It must be confessed that earlier translators have not had this opportunity of using grammar concepts and terms which are of the recent past in understanding Tolkāppiyam. What is most astonishing is that the more recent Higher Grammar terminology most usefully helps the translation effort. There is a leap from the past to the present. The most ancient of Tamil Grammars answers the most recent tools of analysis of modern English Grammar.

There are here, topics suggested for research. A glance at these will show very clearly the wonder Tolkāppiyam is. I leave this ecstacy to the user of this translation who will become eventually the researcher.
