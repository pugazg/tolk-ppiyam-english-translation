Tolkāppiyam seems to treat the theory as different from Data Accounting. All the three terms are used here as technical terms in the science of research methodology. For a discussion of the meaning of these terms one can see Philip W. Davis’s “Preface” in his _Modern Theories of Language_.

What are the implications of these two approaches, one a theory of language the other a theory of research methodology? An awareness of the two seemed to have influenced the Grammarian.

1. There are verses in Tolkāppiyam that are declarative independent in form and general formulae. These are of Grammar, a Linguist’s Grammar.
2. There are verses that are less declarative in tone and dependent on a sense of continuity among the verses. These are of the usages, the mechanics of a language.
3. Omissions, deviations are data, and accounting is made very profitably here. They are in the chapters on usages. The theory is stated in the chapters and verses on Grammar properly understood. There are repeated references to many other usages not mentioned here and a clear awareness of what happens to the grammatical theory when some data do not account. The last two verses of this part so profitably placed there show this awareness of a research theory.

For example chapter 1 gives us the Grammar and chapter 2 the usages and they are differently worded. Both the terms assert this differences. Many difficulties arose for the commentators because they failed to see this distinction between Grammar and grammar. Dr. S. V. Subramanian summarises the difficulties of the early commentators briefly in his _Comparative Study of Grammars_⁴. Omissions of usages, inclusion of hypothetical idealizations and repetitions which they point out as difficulties, deficiencies or dismiss as interpolations in Tolkāppiyam are really the strengths of this monumental work. This becomes clear only when you see what is scientifically meant by theory, data and accounting and their interrelation.

If you co-relate the verses in chapter 1 with those in the next you will see that the first in on Grammar is this helpful sense and the second is on the Usage.
