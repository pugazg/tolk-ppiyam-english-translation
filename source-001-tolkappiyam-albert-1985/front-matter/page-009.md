# 1. The Work

Tolkāppiyam, the great Tamil Grammar is a monumental work and it is not easy to describe in a few words its place in any scheme for Tamil Studies. “Its importance for the study of Tamil culture and of cultures in general cannot be exaggerated.”¹ In three parts it deals with Phonology, Morphology, Syntax and Theory of Literature. There are discussions and disputations about its age. With the help of internal evidence scholars place it between 1000 B. C. and 600 B. C.

Since those who are going to use this translation need only a few fundamental facts about this work, no elaborate discussion of the life of the author or the layout of the work is attempted here. Two articles in English will help very much any one who is going to use this translation. They are _An Introduction to the Study of Tolkāppiyam_² and _Tolkāppiyam - the Earliest Record_³. These are the most basic readings because they provide the reader with the essential facts about the Grammar and at the same time suggest almost all the problems an enthusiastic scholar can pursue to study very profitably. Dr. Ilakkuvanār, in his “Introduction”⁴ to _Tholkappiyam (in English) with Critical studies_ discusses the name of the work, the age, its structure, interpolations and its place in Tamil studies.

In Tamil, the reader will find K. Vellaiyāraṇan’s hundred and seventy two page discussion most useful which the author captions “History of Tamil Literature, Tolkāppiyam.”⁵ The first volume of _Compilation of Commentaries on Tolkāppiyam_⁶ edited by A. Sivalinganar will be of great help too.

For an exhaustive list for reading in English and Tamil on Tolkāppiyam one can refer to Aravanan’s _The Encyclopaedia of Tholkappiyam_⁷ along with the suggestions offered for its improvement in _Annals of Tamil Research_⁸.
