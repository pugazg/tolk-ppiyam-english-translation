# 5. A System of Transliteration

It is not easy to put down the sounds on the Tamil phonology with the help of the International phonetic alphabet with accepted diacritical marks. While this not the place to discuss this problem in detail one should be reminded of the fact that a simpler process would be a transliteration and not transcription. Certainly this involves the learning of the Tamil sounds first and then associating them with the symbols of the English alphabet along with the diacritical marks.

One need not worry about how far the English representative sounds go with the symbols themselves or how far the diacritical marks here are the same as in representation of sounds on the French or Spanish Phonology. The simplest thing is to get the Tamil sounds and get them associated with the English scripts with or without the English scripts with or without the diacritical marks.

Thus learning this system of transliteration is very important.
