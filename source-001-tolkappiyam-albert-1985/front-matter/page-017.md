**Comparative Linguistics:** English is nonphonetic and Tamil is phonetic. This means that the concept of one writing representation for one significant sound unit is more accounted for in Tamil than in English. But this does not concern us here. The system of relation between a representative letter, the written form and sound unit is not of transcription. It can be termed as Exclusive transliteration.⁹

For these reasons we use the term Phonemic Representation in preference to phonetic Representation.

This brief discussion is certainly a call to Tamil scholars to adopt and popularize the I. P. A. symbols with di-critical marks which the world of scholars will certainly accept. Until this is popularized one has to be happy with a system of transliteration.

## V. The Energy Basis of Production of Sounds

Tamil scholars have made useful attempts to give a two term label to Tamil phonemes according to the place and manner of production.¹⁰ They call the hard consonants stops soft consonant Nasals and the medial Continuants. But then there is a tendency to say that some of the labels used in Tamil Grammar are inappropriate and inadequate and hard, soft and medial are used because they are simple and we are used to it.¹¹ This is not a good reaction, I believe.

I shall merely raise a point of order without entering into a full discussion here.

The energy basis which the three terms hard, soft and medial in Tamil denote, is found to make a vital difference in pairing and distinguishing phonemes.
