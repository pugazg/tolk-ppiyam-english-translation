The vertical lines that are used in the text to identify the transliteration are marks of convenience and clarity like the two marks | | and [ ] usually employed in I.P.A. But they don’t give the indications I.P.A. marks give viz., a phoneme and a sound or broad and narrow transcriptions. These are for the sake of clarity.

These thirty one transliteration symbols are adopted from the Transliteration table used in the _Tamil Lexicon_¹.

The six symbols representing sounds on the Sanskrit phonology proper, used there are not used here. This is done in accordance with what is followed at the International Institute of Tamil Studies.²
