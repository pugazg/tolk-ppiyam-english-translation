# 2. Conventions of Usage

## Naming—

### 77

The rest may occur there to name themselves.

## Consonants—

### 78

Only the eleven consonants | ñ |, | ṇ |, | n |, | m |, | ṉ |, | y |, | r |, | l |, | v |, | ḻ | and | ḷ | occur at the final position.

## | ñ | —

### 79

| ñ | occurs as | cu | does.

## | ṉ | —

### 80

| ṉ | behaves like | pu | but does not give the double meaning.

## | v |

### 81

| v | stands at the final position in four words.

## | ṉ | not = | m | —

### 82

There are nine words of the non-personal gender where | ṉ | in the final position cannot be substituted by | m |.
