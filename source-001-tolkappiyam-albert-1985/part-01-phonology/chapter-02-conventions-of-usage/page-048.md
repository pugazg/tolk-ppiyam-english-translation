# 2. Conventions of Usage

## Shorter | u | + —

### 67

The shorter | u | in combination with | n | occurs initially in a word when that word denotes a relationship.

## | u | + —

### 68

Even if this is produced as one full | u | in the same environment the meaning does not change.

## Vowels in the final position

### 69

All vowels except | au | take the final position.

## | au | + —

### 70

| au | occurs there in combination with | k | and | v |.

## | e | + —

### 71

The vowel | e | in combination with the consonants will not take the final position.

## | o | + —

### 72

| o | too behaves in the same manner except with | n |.

## | ē | + and | ō | + —

### 73

The vowels | ē | and | ō | do not occur there in combination with | ñ |.

## | u | + and | ū | + —

### 74

| u | and | ū | do not go with | n | or | v | either.

## | cu | —

### 75

| cu | occurs finally only in two words.

## | pu | —

### 76

There is only one final occurrence of | pu | and it gives both active and passive meaning.
