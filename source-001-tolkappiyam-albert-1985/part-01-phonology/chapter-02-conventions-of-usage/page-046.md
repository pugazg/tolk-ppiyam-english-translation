# 2. Conventions of Usage

## Vowels at the initial position

### 59

All the twelve vowels can take the initial position.

## Vowel-Consonants—

### 60

Consonants without the vowels do not occur at the initial position.

## Consonants—

### 61

The five consonants | k |, | t |, | n |, | p | and | m | can occur at the initial position in combination with all the vowels.

## | c | + —

### 62

| c | follows the same rule except when in combination with | ai |, | au |.

## | v | + —

### 63

The four vowels | u |, | ū |, | o | and | ō | in combination with | v | will never occur initially.

## | ñ | +

### 64

The three vowels | ā |, | e | and | o | may occur there in combination with | ñ |.

## | y | +

### 65

| y | never takes the initial position except with | ā |.

## Naming—

### 66

Those phonemes which do not take the initial position do so for pointing out their names.
