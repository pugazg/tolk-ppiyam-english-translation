# 2. Conventions of Usage

## Vowel-cluster

### 41

When a certain measure is found wanting in a word the corresponding short vowel occurs after the long one to supply this.

## — for diphthongs

### 42

| i | and | u | supply this addition after the two phonemes | ai | and | au |.

## Free morphemes with single phonemes

### 43

Only the seven long vowels occur each as one word.

### 44

None of the five short vowels occurs as one word.

## Three classes of words

### 45

Words are classified in three ways: words of one phonemic representation, words of two representation and a continuum of more than two.

## Production of a consonantal sound

### 46

A consonant is produced only when it makes a combination with | a |.

## Consonant clusters

### 47

Consonants may not follow the set patterns of clustering when they occur independently to name themselves.

## Patterns of clusters

### 48

| y |, | r | and | ḻ | are followed by | k |, | c |, | t |, | p |; | ṅ |, | ñ |, | n | and | m | forming double clusters.

### 49

Of the three | r | and | ḻ | do not occur after short phonemes.
