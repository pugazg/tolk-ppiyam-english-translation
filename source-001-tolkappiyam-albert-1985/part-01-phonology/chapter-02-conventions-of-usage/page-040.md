# 2. Conventions of Usage

## The shorter | i |

### 34

The shorter | i | occurs in combination with the consonant | m | when the bound morpheme | ya | follows this combination.

## — as words join

### 35

The shorter | i | appears at the places where words join and this will be discussed later.

## Shorter | u | in the final position

### 36

The shorter | u | occurs at the final position along with the six hard consonants if the six occur after one long phoneme and after a string of more than one.

## — at word combinations

### 37

In the combination of words too it is shortened and this will be discussed along with the morpho-phonemic rules.

## The place for | k |

### 38

The | āytam | formed with the dots occurs in a word after short phonemes and before the six hard vowel-consonants.

## The lengthened—

### 40

When the lengthened | āytam | occurs in onomatopoeic words representing shape and sound, which is rather rare, this length cannot be represented in writing.
