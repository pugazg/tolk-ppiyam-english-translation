# 9. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with the Shorter | u |

## Two of them

### 414

Scholars tell us that in the sequences after a medial consonant and the | āytam | the same usage is followed.

## Another two

### 415

Following a hard consonantal sequence and a soft consonantal sequence, the hard consonant leaves its recurrence inbetween. In soft consonantal sequences this soft consonant is kept or the corresponding hard consonant takes this place.

## —trees

### 416

These names of trees take the empty morph | am |.

### 417

There are names of trees where the soft consonants are not replaced by the hard ones.

## Two of them

### 418

There are some words among the words of two letters and the hard consonantal sequences which take | am |. This can be found from the usage.

## Empty morphs

### 419

There are words among these where the consonant does not change when it takes | akku |.

## Numbers

### 420

Names of numbers follow the rules for case markers.

## Two words

### 421

| vaṇṭu | and | peṇṭu | take | iṉ |.

## One of them

### 422

They do not rule out | aṉ | for the word | peṇṭu |.
