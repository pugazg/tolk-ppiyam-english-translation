# 9. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with the Shorter | u |

## Certain words

### 449

The same holds good when words with | k |, | c |, | t | and | p | at the initial position come to join,

## Eight

### 450

The same changes take place when | eṭṭu | is followed by the three | n |, | m |, | v | and | a |.

## Five, three

### 451

| aintu | and | mūṉṟu | replace their consonants by the consonants of the following words | n | and | m | that stand at the initial position of these words.

## Three

### 452

| mūṉṟu | replaces its consonants by the consonant in the | va | that follows it.

## Four

### 453

The consonant in | nāṉku | is replaced by | l |.

## Five

### 454

The consonant in | aintu | disappears as in the previous cases.⁴⁰

## One, two

### 455

It is said that when a vowel follows the first two numbers the | u | disappears and their sounds at the initial position are lengthened.

## Three Four Five

### 456

In the words | mūṉṟu |, | nāṉku | and | aintu | with a following | v | the changes occur as shown earlier.

## Three

### 457

The initial sound in | mūṉṟu | is lengthened if the word | uḻakku | follows in usage.
