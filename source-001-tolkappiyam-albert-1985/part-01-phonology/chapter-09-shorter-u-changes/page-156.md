# 9. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with the Shorter | u |

## Two

### 440

The | ra | that stands in the middle in | iraṇṭu | disappears in this realization.

## Three, six

### 441

The long initial sounds of | mūṉṟu | and | āṟu | will shorten and the consonant of | mūṉṟu | will become | p |.

## Four

### 442

The consonant in | nāṉku | becomes | ṟ |.

## Five

### 443

The consonant in | aintu | becomes | m |.

## Eight

### 444

The consonant in | eṭṭu | becomes | ṇ |.

## Nine

### 445

The | o | of | oṉ | assimilates | t |, the following consonant is replaced by a recurrence of | ṇ | and in the word | paktu | the | āytam | and | pa | disappear, the | u | is kept while the consonant | t | becomes | ṟ |.

## Measures, weights

### 446

When names of measures and weights follow these, they follow the same rule.

## Three

### 447

The consonant in | mūṉṟu | is replaced by the consonant in the word that follows.

## Five

### 448

The consonant of | aintu | is replaced by the corresponding hard consonant.
