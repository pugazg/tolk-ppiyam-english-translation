# Notes

## 37

This verse offers a considerable amount of difficulty for the commentators. They question the very need for such a direction in this verse. The grammarian speaks about the label we give in such occurrence with two consonants. One might think that since the hard consonant is produced as a result of the preceding medial one and the following phoneme joining in a situation of morphophonemic change, this hard consonant is of secondary importance and the label can go by the preceding medial consonant. The Grammarian says that it should not be so. In a Tamil word like | irku | the name goes by the consonant | k | and not by | r | though | k | is the result of the coming together of | r | and | u | in a morphophonemic situation.
