# 9. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with the Shorter | u |

## Directions

### 432

If two words naming directions join, then | ē | occurs inbetween.

## Medial directions

### 433

Scholars tell us that for the medial directions the final sound and the preceding consonant disappear. When any word joins | teṟku | the consonant gives place to | ṉ |.

## Numerals

### 434

In all the names of numbers from | oṉṟu | to | eṭṭu | when they follow | pattu | the shorter | u | along with its consonant disappears and except in the case of two in all other cases | iṉ | appears.

## +consonant

### 435

It is said that when | iraṇṭu | follows then the consonants too disappear and there is a recurrence of | ṉ |.

## Thousand

### 436

With | āyiram | too these rules hold good.

## Weights, measures

### 437

With the names of weights and measures too the empty morph | iṉ | appears.

## Numerals

### 438

When the names of numbers from | oṉṟu | to | oṉpatu | are followed by | pattu | the consonant disappears in this and the | āytam | takes this place. In these names the shorter | u | now exists only at the final position of | āṟu |.³⁹

## Two, three

### 439

The consonant in the first two numbers become | r |; and | u | joins it.
