# 9. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with the Shorter | u |

## —

### 468

The consonant in | aintu | is replaced by | y |.

## —

### 469

The shorter | u | in | āṟu | must disappear leaving there its consonant.

## —

### 470

The final in | oṉpatu | will not change and according to tradition it takes the empty morph | iṉ |.

## Ten Thousand

### 471

When | nūṟāyiram | follows, the preceding numerals behave as | nūṟu | does.

## Hundred +

### 472

When the word | nūṟu | is followed by the names of numbers one to nine, the | u | in the final position disappears and its consonant recurs.

## + ten

### 473

The same change occurs there when they are followed by | pattu |.

## Measures weights

### 474

The same holds good with names of measures and weights. Scholars think that the shorter | u | and the occurrence of the consonant follow the usages described earlier.

## + ten

### 475

The consonant in the middle gets doubled as the | āytam | drops in ten when it follows the numerals starting from one and when the numerals one to nine occur following this.
