# 9. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with the Shorter | u |

## Places

### 407

There are six places where the shorter | u | occurs. They occur in a word of two letters and in sequences after a vowel, a medial consonant, an | āytam |, a hard consonant and a soft consonant.

## Two consonants

### 408

If there are two consonants in a sequence it will not go by the name of the medial consonant.³⁷

## Case, non-case

### 409

In all such final position in case and non-case relations the | u | gets shortened.

## Zero

### 410

If in a sequence the hard consonant is to recur because of a hard consonantal initial sound in a following word, this shortening may not occur.

## The shorter | i |

### 411

As the | u | disappears completely, the | i | gets shortened when | y | follows it.

## Two of them

### 412

This word of two letters and this sequence after a vowel in case relations get a hard consonant recurrence because of a hard consonantal initial sound in the following word.

## Zero

### 413

There are places where there is no consonantal recurrence; nor does the hard consonant appear there.
