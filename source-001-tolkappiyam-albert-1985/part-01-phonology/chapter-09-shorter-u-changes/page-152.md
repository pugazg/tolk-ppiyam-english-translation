# 9. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with the Shorter | u |

## With demonstratives

### 423

The final sound in | yātu | and the final in an | āytam | sequence with an initial demonstrative follow the usages of case markers.

## Non-case

### 424

In non-case relations when this is followed by a vowel, the dotted | āytam | is retained there.

## Different sounds

### 425

This | āytam | has no place if other sounds follow.

## Non-case

### 426

In non-case relations all these utterances go according to the usages described already.

## One of them

### 427

After a hard consonantal sequence, the following hard consonant recurs.

## Another

### 428

In a soft consonantal sequence where the demonstrative part is lengthened or where it is an interrogative | yā | the usage of hard consonantal recurrence is kept.

## — | yā |

### 429

The utterance with | yā | may remain without a change.

## Four

### 430

Those four³⁸ utterances do not change.

## —exists

### 431

When the word | untu | stands for the quality of being existent this particular sound along with its consonant drops as the preceding consonant becomes | ḷ |. These two happen when a hard consonant follows this word.
