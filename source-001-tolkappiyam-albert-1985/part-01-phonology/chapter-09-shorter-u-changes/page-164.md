# 9. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with the Shorter | u |

## +thousand

### 476

When | āyiram | follows these the consonant is not doubled as the empty morph | iṉ | is introduced.

## Measures, weights.

### 477

With measures and weights the same pattern is followed.

## First

### 478

In the opinion of scholars when the hard consonants, | ñ |, | n |, | m | and | y |, | v | follow the first number, the pattern discussed earlier holds good.

## + vowel

### 479

In these words when a vowel or | u | follows them, the initial | o | becomes | ō | and the | u | in | ru | disappears.

## + mā

### 480

If the morpheme | mā | in its meaning in common usage follows the words two to nine then the same changes applicable to a word of measurement with an initial | m | occur.

## Versification

### 481

After the consonants | l | and | n | at the final position of words, morphemes like | um | and | keḻu | appear in case relations with words giving them meaning in verses.

## Other changes

### 482

There are irregular and defective words born of suggestiveness of qualification and of onomatopoeic ending in vowels and consonants, the adjectives with particles of the five gender⁴¹ endings in both personal and non-personal groups, adjectives | ceyum | and | ceyta | which form verbal phrases, the same numerals coming one after the other in one series and all such words that have undergone irregular changes in usage are accepted. These cannot be shown here in the various steps in the process of change.
