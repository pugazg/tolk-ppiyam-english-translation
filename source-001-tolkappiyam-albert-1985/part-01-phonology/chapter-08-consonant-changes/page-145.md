# Notes

## 30

The word “fully” is introduced by the author because this phoneme at the final position is likely to take other vowels and so the whole of this formation is dropped.

## 31

A corresponding consonant will mean soft one that corresponds on the alphabet to the initial sound that follows.

## 32

This | a | will mean the | a | of | am | which is referred to in this continuum of verses.

Obvious reference to verse 311.

## 36

The Tamil word used here in the original is a picturesque epithet. It means alternate. This word was translated in V. 243 as “may drop or may not”. Here the equivalent is used without filling in the case gaps in translation. The phrase “either way” in V. 369 is another translation of the same word.
