# 8. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with Consonants

### Two words

## 375

When | nāy | and | palakai | follow this, the | u | may disappear and when this happens | a | takes this place.

### Three words

## 376

| am | is inserted after the three words | pūl |, | vēl | and | āl |.

### Gerunds

## 377

All these behave like the other gerunds.

### | veyil |

## 378

The word | veyil | behaves like | maḻai |.

### | v |

## 379

A word with a demonstrative at the initial and a | v | at the final position behaves as it does when followed by case markers, in the manner shown already.

### Non-case

## 380

This becomes an | aytam | in non-case relations.

### + soft

## 381

If a soft consonant comes to join, then the same consonant recurs.

### The other consonants

## 382

It is said that with the other consonants there is no change.

### Other places

## 383

The | v | in other environments behaves like the gerunds.
