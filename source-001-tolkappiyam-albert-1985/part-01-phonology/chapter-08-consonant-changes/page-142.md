# 8. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with Consonants

### Hundred

## 393

With the word | āyiram | preceded by | nūṟu | the shortening of the long initial sound mentioned above does not take place.

### + words = numbers

## 394

The same happens when words denoting numbers and not things ending in | ai |, | am | and | pal | follow.

### + vowel

## 395

This rule holds good even when a vowel follows.

### Alternation

## 396

The word | kīḻ | appears either way.

### | ḷ |

## 397

A final | ḷ | behaves like a | ṇ | of the same position.

### Shift to | ṇ |

## 398

This becomes | ṇ | when the soft consonant comes to join.

### Non-case

## 399

In all non-case relations it behaves either way.

### + | t |

## 400

When | t | occurs the appearance of | aytam | is not ruled out.

## 401

There are utterances where this, along with a preceding long phoneme, admits no change and where words in non-case relations behave like those in case relations.
