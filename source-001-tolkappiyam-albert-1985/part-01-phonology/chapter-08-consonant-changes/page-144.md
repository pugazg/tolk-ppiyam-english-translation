# 8. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with Consonants

### —gerunds

## 402

All these gerunds behave like the other gerunds.

### — = | veyil | .

## 403

The word | iruḷ | behaves like | veyil |.

### —two words

## 404

| puḷ | and | vaḷ | behave like the gerunds.

### —name

## 405

The naming word | makkaḷ | is given a hard consonant ending in suitable places as we recognize them in usage.

### Other usages

## 406

What are found in usage must also be taken into consideration besides what are listed here as morphophonemic rules.
