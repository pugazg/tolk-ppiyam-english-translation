### 10. Distinctive Feature Approach to Phonology in Tolkāppiyam

One of the well known approaches to phonology in the west is the Distinctive Feature approach. When this is applied to Tolkāppiyam it will reveal the inclusive nature of this grammar which seeks to make use of available grammars of the time.

Basic reading: _Applications of Linguistics_, ed. G. E. Perren and J. L. M. Trim (London: Cambridge University Press, 1971).
