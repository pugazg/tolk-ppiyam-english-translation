# 7. Research Topics

Here are ten research topics on Tolkāppiyam: Part I Phonology. Hints are given here and they should be made use of for making hard and richly rewarding findings. Topics in the first series are already referred to. They are gathered here for convenience and the rest are new ones.

A basic reading is suggested for each topic. Only the relevant portions need to be read to begin with from these texts.

## I

### 1. The Basic Research Methodology of Tolkāppiyam

Basic Reading: R. Karl, _The Logic of Scientific Discovery_ (New York: Basic Books, Inc., 1959)

### 2. Theoretical and Practical Implications of the Linguistic Grammar and the Mechanics of Language in Tolkāppiyam

Basic reading: Noam Chomsky, “Conditions on Rules of Grammar,” _Current Issues in Linguistic Theory_, ed. Roger Cole 1977.

### 3. Modern Linguistic Theories and Tolkāppiyam

Basic reading: Philip W. Davis, _Modern Theories of Language_, 1973.

### 4. The Textuality of Tolkāppiyam and its Implications to the Commentators

Basic reading: Paul C. Wermuth, _Modern Essays on Writing and Style_, 2nd Edn. (New York: Holt, Rinehart and Winston, Inc., 1964).
