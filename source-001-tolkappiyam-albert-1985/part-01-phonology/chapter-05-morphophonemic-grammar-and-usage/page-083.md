# Notes

## 15a

Among these thirty verses you will find verses that are declarative and independent. These are of Grammar. There are also verses which give the usage. So we title this chapter as Grammar and Usage. The Tamil title of the original is translated in the light of this suitably.

In the previous Chapter which is a Chapter on Grammar you may suspect there are verses that talk about individual sounds. It has to be so because they talk about changes of sounds. But they are declarative and independent mostly hence we use the word Grammar.

## 16

The words personal and non-personal class should not mislead the English reader who uses the word person with the words First, Second and Third. Personal here means Human and Non-personal Non-human. Since the use of human and nonhuman may put the stress on certain qualities, we shall use the word person which for the English reader gives the idea of grammar categories. But he should avoid equating the significance. In English the third person contains he, she, it and they. Therefore it has both the personal and the non-personal. He,she, they are personal and it, they are the non-personal.

A useful reading will be the second chapter in Pon. Kothandaraman, _Linguistic Studies in Tamil_ (Madras : Tamil Nulagam, 1960).

We have the three personal pronoun slots in Tamil -I, you and he. Therefore in 152 and 153 the phrase second person is used to denote the you slot as in English.

## 17

One must look at the process of doubling in Tamil carefully. In some places, here, it is called the recurrence, imitating the Italian equivalent “raddoppiamento sinattico” which is very illustrative.
