# 5. Conventions of Morphophonemic Grammar and Usage

## | t |, | n | shift

### 150

| t | and | n | become | ṟ | and | ṉ | if they follow the consonants | l | and | ṇ |.

### 151

They become | ṭ | and | ṇ | after | ṇ | and | ḷ |.

## Verb with the second personal ending

### 152

The final vowel or consonant in a second personal verb is realized with or without change when the hard consonants are at the initial position in the following word.

### 153

This will not be applicable strictly to words of the second person ending in | au | or in, | ñ |, | ṇ |, | m |, | y | or the shorter | u |.

## Personal Class nouns¹⁶

### 154

In all types of relations, it is said, personal class nouns ending in vowels or in consonants undergo no change.

## Noun with | i |

### 155

Among them there are places where the noun ending in | i | may take a change.

## Personal-nonpersonal nouns

### 156

Those personal class nouns that are used in the place of non-personal class nouns may remain unchanged.
