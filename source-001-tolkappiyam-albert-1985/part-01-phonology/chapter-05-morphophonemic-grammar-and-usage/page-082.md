# 5. Conventions of Morphophonemic Grammar and Usage

## Rules from usage

### 172

The various rules applicable to the changes in the final and the following sounds in words are to be framed in accordance with the right usage.

## Deformed usage

### 173

The disappearance of | va | in the word | yāvar | when it follows a word denoting many persons and the introduction of | va | in | yātu | when it follows a non-personal class singular pronoun, are classified as two deformed usages.
