# 5. Conventions of Morphophonemic Grammar and Usage¹⁵ᵃ

## Consonant shift

### 144

The soft consonants | ṅ |, | ñ |, | n | and | m | are realised when they precede | k |, | c |, | t | and | p | respectively as found in the alphabet system.

## No change

### 145

If the initial sound of the following word is | ñ |, | n |, | m |, | y |, | v | or a vowel no change takes place whatever be the sound at the final position of the preceding word and whatever be the relationship between the two words.

## In words with more than two letters

### 146

At the end of words of more than two letters, the soft consonants may also be differently realized.

## With verbs

### 147

Scholars tell us that | y | and | ñ | may stand at the initial position in verbs after the words with consonants | ṇ | and | ṉ | at the final position.

## No change in non case

### 148

If these two consonants are at the final position of a word and the following word introduces any of thos, that can be initial sounds, then there is no changee provided it is a non-case relation.

## —

### 149

Even in case relations the same applies with these two consonants except when the hard consonants are involved.
