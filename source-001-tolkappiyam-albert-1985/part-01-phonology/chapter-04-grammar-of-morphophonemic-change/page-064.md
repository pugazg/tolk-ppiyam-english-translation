# 4. A Chapter on the Grammar of Morphophonemic Change

## Personal/non-personal nouns

### 118

Nouns in as much as they point out names are of two classes, the personal and the non-personal.

## The place for the empty morph

### 119

Empty morphs are added to these after them.

## Empty morphs

### 120

There are a number of empty morphs such as | in |, | vaṟṟu |, | attu |, | am |, | on |, | ān |, | akku |, | ikku |, and | an |.

## Elision of | i |

### 121

Of these the | i | of | in | may disappear after a final | ā |.

## Assimilation of | ṉ |

### 122

The | ṉ | too may change into | r | when it precedes a word meaning measure.

## Elision of | v |.

### 123

After the demonstrative where | a | is followed by | i | the consonant | v | is dropped and the | a | is retained.

## Assimilation of | ṉ |

### 124

The | ṉ | become | r | when the fourth case marker follows.

### 125

When a noun naming a day takes | ān | and when this is followed by a verb with a hard consonant at its initial position the ṉ undergoes the same change.

## Elision of | a |

### 126

The | a | in | attu | disappears when this follows | a |.
