# 4. A Chapter on the Grammar of Morphophonemic Change

## Phonemes in the initial and final positions

### 104

Of the thirty three sounds twenty two occur initially, twenty four occur finally and all words begin and end only with two, the consonant and the vowel.

## Final consonant’s shape

### 105

Of these, the final consonants will take dots.

## Shorter | u |

### 106

The shorter | u | too gets the same.

## Vowel-consonant in the final position

### 107

A vowel-consonant ending behaves as a final vowel does.

## The four situations : Phonemes

### 108

In the study of morphophonemic changes the preceding and the following words are involved in four ways; the vowel may follow a vowel ending, a consonant may follow a vowel ending, a vowel may follow a consonant ending or a consonant may follow a consonant ending.
