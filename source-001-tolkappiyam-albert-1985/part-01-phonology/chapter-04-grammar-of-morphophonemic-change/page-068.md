# 4. A Chapter on the Grammar of Morphophonemic Change

## Elision + addition

### 134

It is understood that the consonant that precedes the two words | attu | and | vaṟṟu | disappear, and the hard consonants that follow them are doubled.

## Three more empty morphs.

### 135

| kāram |, | karam | and | kān | are also empty morphs.

## Position of—

### 136

Of these | karam | and | kān | are not added to long phonemes.

## —

### 137

These three morphs are known to be added to the short phonemes.

## —

### 138

| ai | and | au | may also occur along with | kān |.

## Vowel consonant formation

### 139

A vowel disappears when it follows a final consonant and merges with that consonant.

## —division

### 140

When the cansonant frees itself from the vowel it receives its own form.

## The intrusive consonant

### 141

In all formations if a vowel is followed by a vowel the use of an appropriate intrusive consonant is not forbidden.
