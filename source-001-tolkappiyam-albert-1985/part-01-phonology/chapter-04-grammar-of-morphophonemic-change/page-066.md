# 4. A Chapter on the Grammar of Morphophonemic Change

## — | i |

### 127

The | i | in | ikku | disappears when this follows | i |.

### 128

It is the same when it follows | ai |.

## —shorter | u | +

### 129

When | akku | follows a noun and this is followed by a word with an initial hard consonant the shorter | u | in | akku | is dropped and its consonant along with the preceding one disappears.

## Assimilation of | k |, | c |, | t |

### 130

In | am | with the | m | at the final position, the | k |, | c | and | t | that follow, change to | ṅ | | ñ | and | n |.

## Elision of | m |

### 131

Scholars tell us that the same disappears when it is followed by the soft and the medial sounds.

## | in | mutually exclusive

### 132

The case marker | in | does not take the empty morph | in |.

## Empty Morph’s position

### 133

When a noun and a verb occur side by side, the empty morph is placed between the base and the case marker as in the rules, or put after the base when the case marker is not needed and when the noun and the verb are used as distinctive units the empty morph disappears. As regards | oṭu | it makes no difference whether it appears or not.
