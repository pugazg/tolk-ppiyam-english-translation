# 4. A Chapter on the Grammar of Morphophonemic Change

## —in the two categories

### 109

When the initial sound of the phrase that follows joins the final sound of the preceding phrase, a noun may go with a noun, a noun may go with a verb, may go with a noun and a verb with a verb. In three of the four there will be changes and in one no change.

## Three types of changes

### 110

These changes are assimilation, addition and elision.

## With adjuncts

### 111

Even if there are adjuncts added to the preceding and the following words they behave in the same manner.

## —deformed words

### 112

Even if the words are deformed when they come together the same rules of change hold good.

## Addition as change.

### 113

In the context of a case relation as well as a non-case relation, a phoneme or an empty morph may appear when morphophonemic changes take place.

## Case markers

### 114

| ai |, | otu |, | ku |, | in |, | atu | and | kan | are the six case-markers.

## Consonant recurrence

### 115

Case markers which have the hard consonants at the initial positions must have the recurrence of consonants suitably.

## The sixth case marker

### 116

The | a | of the sixth case marker must be dropped when it follows a word with a final | a |.

## The place for the—

### 117

Case markers are placed after the noun.
