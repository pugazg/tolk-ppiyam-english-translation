# 3. A Chapter on the Production of Sounds

## Consonants; | k | and | ṅ |

### 89

| k | and | ṅ | are produced by the contact of the tongue with the palate.

## | c | and | ñ |

### 90

| c | and | ñ | are produced by the contact of the middle of the tongue with the palate.

## | ṭ | and | ṇ |

### 91

| ṭ | and | ṇ | are produced as the tip of the tongue touches the palate.

## — three places

### 92

Thus the six phonemes have three places of production.

## — | t | and | n |

### 93

| t | and | n | are produced by the tip of the tongue spreading out and touching the place where the teeth join the palate.

## — | ṟ | and | ṉ |

### 94

| ṟ | and | ṉ | are produced as the tip of the tongue touches the palate in a raised position.

## — | r | and | ḻ |

### 95

| r | and | ḻ | are produced as the tip of the tongue strokes the palate in a raised position.

## — | l | and | ḷ |

### 96

When the tongue swells in its sides, if the tip of the tongue touches the teeth ridge | l | is produced and if it strokes it | ḷ | is produced.
