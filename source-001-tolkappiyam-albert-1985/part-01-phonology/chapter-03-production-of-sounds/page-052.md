# 3. A Chapter on the Production of Sounds

## Air stream passage and vocal organs

### 83

A careful study shows that all the different sounds are produced with the help of the eight parts, as the egressive air stream starting from those parts with navel as the base gets modified in the head,¹² throat and chest and articulated by the teeth, lips, tongue, nose and palate.

## Vowel production

### 84

In this way the twelve vowels are produced as the air stream passes through the throat without any obstruction.

## Open—

### 85

Of the twelve | a | and | ā | are produced as the mouth opens.

## Close—

### 86

The five vowels | i |, | ī |, | e |, | ē | and | ai | are produced in the same manner and¹³ as the edges of the front of the tongue touch the teeth ridge.

## Rounded—

### 87

The five | u |, | o |, | ū |, | ō | and | au | are produced with the rounding of lips.

## Allophones¹⁴

### 88

The variants of these phonemes are not of much significance.¹⁵
