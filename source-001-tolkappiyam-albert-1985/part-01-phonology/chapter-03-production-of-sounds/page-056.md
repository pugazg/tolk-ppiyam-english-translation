# 3. A Chapter on the Production of Sounds

## — | p | and | m |

### 97

| p | and | m | are produced by the contact of the lips.

### 98

— | v | is produced by the contact of the teeth and the lips.

## — | y |

### 99

| y | is produced as the air coming into the throat brushes against the palate.

## soft—; nasalization.

### 100

Though the six soft consonants have their places of production in the places indicated, the manner in which they are shaped depends on the way air escapes through the nose.

## The three

### 101

The three sounds which are found to be dependent or which cannot occur on their own have their places of production closely identified each with that of the primary sound of its combination, maintaining a realisation of its own.

## A complete description

### 102

It is in the learned treatise we find a clear description of how the pulmonic air stream gets modified, as it passes into the various sound units and what measure of duration they take.

## —

### 103

Omitting such complete description I merely make statements about the way the pulmonic air stream is shaped meaningfully into sounds.
