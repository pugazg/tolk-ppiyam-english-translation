# 7. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with Vowels

### Drops

## 289

Scholars tell us that in verse, the final | ai | and the consonant in | vēṭkai | drop if this is followed by | avā | but the | ṭ | must be replaced by | ṇ |.

### | ō |

## 290

A final | ō | behaves like a final | ē |.

### No consonant

## 291

The hard consonant discussed does not occur with this morpheme as it specifies contradiction, interrogation and doubt.

### In negation

## 292

When specifying a negation too, it behaves as it is shown above.

### Case

## 293

It is the same in case relations; here | o | occurs.

### + | il |

## 294

When produced with | il | there is no change.

### As with case markers

## 295

There are words among these which behave the way they behave with case markers. Then there is no recurrence of the hard consonant.

### | au |

## 296

In case and non-case relations it is not wrong for the hard consonant to recur after nouns with | au | at the final position. Great scholars tell us that in both cases it is right for the | u | to occur.
