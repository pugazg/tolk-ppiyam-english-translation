# 7. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with Vowels

## — | pū |

## 269

This change does not happen in the case of the one name | pū |. There, even the hard consonant may occur.

## — + | ā |

## 270

The naming word | ū | joins | ā |.

## — + empty morph

## 271

The proper place where it may take the empty morph | akku | can be found from the usage.

## — + | iṉ | +

## 272

It is not wrong that | iṉ | appears inbetween in the two naming words | āṭūu | and | makaṭūu |.

## | e |, | o |

## 273

| e | and | o | do not take the final position in naming words except in the second person unless here it shows certainty and distinctiveness.

## with—

## 274

The hard consonant described above does not occur with the | e | for certainty and the | o | for distinctiveness.

## — | ē |

## 275

A final | ē | behaves like the final | ū |.

## —

## 276

When denoting contradiction, question or enumeration this hard consonant does not occur.

## —case

## 277

It is the same in case relations too.

## — + | e |

## 278

A final | ē | is followed by | e |.
