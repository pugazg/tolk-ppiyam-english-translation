# 7. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with Vowels

## —in certain categories

## 211

The comparative particle | aṉṉa |, the vocative used with a sense of nearness, the infinite | ceymaṉa |, the imperative expressing a wish, the premodifier in a noun phrase²⁴ | ceyta|, the premodifier in a verbal phase | ceyyiya |, the intrusive morpheme²⁵ | ama | and words denoting the many with a final | a | remain unchanged.

## —

## 212

The final | ya | of | vāliya | in the sense of wishing a long life may be dropped.

## —in | ama |

## 213

It is not wrong to lengthen the intrusive morpheme.

## —the many

## 214

Phonemes at the final position of words which denote the many may be lengthened in formations in versification.

## — + | l |

## 215

The final | la | may change to | ra | in words of two letters when the same word follows.

## —

## 216

The change into hard consonant is optional.

## — case relations

## 217

The same holds good in case relations too.

## — tree names

## 218

In the words naming trees the soft consonant appears.
