# 7. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with Vowels

### In certain categories

## 211

The comparative particle | anna |, the vocative used with a sense of nearness, the infinitive | ceymana |, the imperative expressing a wish, the premodifier in a noun phrase²⁴ | ceyta |, the premodifier in a verbal phrase | ceyyiya |, the intrusive morpheme²⁵ | amma | and words denoting the many with a final | a | remain unchanged.

## 212

The final | ya | of | vāḻiya | in the sense of wishing a long life may be dropped.

### In | amma |

## 213

It is not wrong to lengthen the intrusive morpheme.

### The many

## 214

Phonemes at the final position of words which denote the many may be lengthened in formations in versification.

## 215

The final | l | may change to | r | in words of two letters when the same word follows.

## 216

The change into hard consonant is optional.

### Case relations

## 217

The same holds good in case relations too.

### Tree names

## 218

In the words naming trees the soft consonant appears.
