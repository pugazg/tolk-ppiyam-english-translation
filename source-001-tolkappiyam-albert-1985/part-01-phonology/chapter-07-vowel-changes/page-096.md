# 7. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage with Vowels²¹

## 204

If the consonants | k |, | c |, | t | and | p | follow a noun with an | a | at the final position the consonant at the initial position is doubled if there is a non-case relation.

## 205

In the premodifier in a verbal phrase²², in the comparative particles, in the elliptical | ena | after the demonstrative and in the non-semantic intrusive²³ | āṅka | the hard consonants mentioned above are doubled.

### Demonstrative

## 206

If the demonstrative is followed by | ñ |, | n | and | m | the corresponding consonant is doubled.

## 207

If | y | and | v | follow, | v | occurs inbetween.

## 208

Even when a vowel follows the same rule holds good.

## 209

The lengthened form becomes appropriate in versification.

### With | v |

## 210

The final | va | in | cāva | of the infinitive | ceya | may disappear.
