# 1. Conventions Of Grammar

## Vowel-consonants

### 17

All the consonantal letters without the dots represent the particular sounds pronounced along with | a | and they are vocalized accordingly when they join the other vowels.

## Making a vowel-consonant

### 18

The vowel appears at the place after the consonant.⁹

## Hard consonants¹⁰

### 19

| k |, | c |, | ṭ |, | t |, | p | and | ṟ | are the hard sounds.

## Soft consonants

### 20

| ṅ |, | ñ |, | ṇ |, | n |, | m | and | ṉ | are the soft sounds.

## Medial Consonants

### 21

| y |, | r |, | l |, | v |, | ḻ | and | ḷ | are medial sounds.

## Consonant clusters

### 22

These eighteen consonants form clusters in two ways, either each with itself or one with the other.

## In the second way

### 23

The consonants | ṭ |, | r |, | ḻ | and | ḷ | are followed by | k |, | c | and | p |.

### 24

Of these four | ḻ | and | ḷ | can also be followed by | y | and | v |.

### 25

The consonants | ṅ |, | ñ |, | ṇ |, | n |, | m | and | ṉ | are followed by the corresponding hard consonants.
