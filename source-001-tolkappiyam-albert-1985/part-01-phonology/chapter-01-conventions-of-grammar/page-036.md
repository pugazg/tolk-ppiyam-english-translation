# 1. Conventions Of Grammar

### 26

Among these | ṇ | and | ḷ | are also followed by the seven consonants | k |, | c |, | ñ |, | p |, | m |, | y | and | v |.

### 27

The consonants | ñ |, | n |, | m | and | v | are followed by | y | meaningfully.

### 28

The consonant | m | is followed by | v |

### 29

The consonants | y |, | r | and | ḻ | are followed by the initial consonants¹¹ and | ṅ |

## In the first way

### 30

All the consonants except | r | and | ḻ | form clusters each with itself.

## Demonstrative

### 31

| a |, | i | and | u | are the demonstrative particles.

## Interrogative

### 32

| ā |, | ē | and | ō | make the interrogative.

## Quantity in music

### 33

Scholars tell us that the increase beyond the set quantity of vowels and consonants is described in works on vocal and instrumental music.
