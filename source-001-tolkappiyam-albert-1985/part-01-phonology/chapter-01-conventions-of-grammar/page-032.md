# 1. Conventions Of Grammar

## The vowels

### 8

The twelve phonemes with | au | at the end of the series are called vowels.

## The consonants

### 9

The eighteen phonemes with | ṉ | at the end of the series are called consonants.⁷

## Length: vowel - consonant

### 10

The quantity of the vowel is not altered even when it is produced along with the consonant.

## —: a consonant

### 11

A consonant has half a measure.

## —: the three sounds

### 12

Those three dependent occurrences also have half a measure each.

## —: the shorter | m |

### 13

The quantity in | m |⁸ is further reduced to a half of its measure when | m | is produced along with other sounds; but this occurrence is rare.

## The shape of the shorter | m |

### 14

Its shape is made out with a dot within it.

## —consonant

### 15

The consonants take a dot.

## — | e |, | o |

### 16

The nature of | e | and | o | is also the same.
