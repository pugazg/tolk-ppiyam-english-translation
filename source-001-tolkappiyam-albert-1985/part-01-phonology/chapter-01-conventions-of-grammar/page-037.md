6. Elongation and not length is used here because it is not any length that is meant but the one measure of a short vowel.

7. The English reader knows that vowel or consonants is described according to the air stream mechanism. But these are descriptions and not definitions. In Tamil | uyir | vowel stands for breath or life and | mey | consonant for the body. This naming is rather etymological. Breath creates the phonetic action and the body has no action without the breath. In English vowel is from Fr. Voyelle or L. Vocalis, vox, vocis voice and consonant is from L. Conconsare sound sounded together with a vowel. So the naming concepts are the same.
