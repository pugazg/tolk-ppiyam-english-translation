# 6. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage With Case Markers

## — | iṉ |

### 196

The empty morph | iṉ | follows the shorter | u | in the final position in words to complete it.

## —Recurrence

### 197

The consonant is doubled after a long vowel but not in these words.

## No—with—

### 198

These remain unchang.d.

## — | taṉ | + numerals.

### 199

Endings in numerals take | aṉ |.

### 200

In all the numerals starting in one when used along with ten, the occurrence of | āṉ | is quite right. But there | aktu | disappears and the consonant | p | is kept.

## + | aṉ |

### 201

The sound in the final position in both | yātu | and the demonstrative with the | āytam | takes | aṉ | and the | āytam | itself disappears.

## Directions

### 202

Words showing directions when they are followed by the seventh case marker may or may not take the empty morph; this ending along with the consonant drops.

## Other usages

### 203

All words ending in a consonant or a vowel other than those discussed so far show, on a careful examination that they take case markers but not necessarily along with empty morphs.
