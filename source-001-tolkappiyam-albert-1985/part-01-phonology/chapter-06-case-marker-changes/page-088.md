# 6. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage With Case Markers

## —Consonant | ṉ |

### 180

## — | oṉ |

### 181

## — | attu | 7th case

### 182

## — | iṉ |

### 183

## — | vaṟṟu |

### 184

### 185

### 186
