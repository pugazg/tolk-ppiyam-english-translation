# 6. A Chapter on the Changes in Usage With Case Markers¹⁹

## The empty morph | iṉ |

### 174

If words have the following six phonemes | a |, | ā |, | u |, | ū |, | e | and | au | at the final position whan the case markers follow, the empty morph used is | iṉ |.

## — | vaṟṟu |

### 175

Nouns denoting the many with | a | at the final position take | vaṟṟu | without fail.

## —

### 176

The interrogative | yā | does not fail to follow this usage either.

## — | aṉ |

### 177

In words with an initial demonstrative the following | u | takes | aṉ | making its consonantal part disappear along with the loss of | u |.

## — | vaṟṟu |

### 178

Words with an initial demonstrative and a final | ai | take | vaṟṟu |.

## —

### 179

Scholars tell us that the final | ai | too in the interrogative | yā | follows this rule without fail; the | v | disappears along with the | ai |.
