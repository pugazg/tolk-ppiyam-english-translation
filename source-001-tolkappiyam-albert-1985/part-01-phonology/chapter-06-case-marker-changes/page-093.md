# Notes

## 19

You must understand the scheme of this chapter. Then you will understand the verses very well. This is a chapter on usage not on the grammar of case markers. This grammar was given in Section 4. So, naturally you don’t find here what happens to the case-markers but what happens to the preceding unit at the final position as the markers follow them. That is why you find an elaborate discussion of the changes that happen in the empty morph. You have been told already in Section 4 the relative positions of the case marker and the empty morph.

You need not be disturbed by the number of verses devoted to these changes. Remeber that in a strict scientific sense of research methodology data is what is of interest. Why should Noam Chomsky speak so much on the passive construction? Some people say that we don’t use the passive construction so very often. These people are wrong. Data is what is of interest. See any useful treatise on research methodology like G. Carl Hempel, _Philosophy of Natural Science_ (Englewood Cliffs, N. J. : Prentice-Hall, Inc., 1966)
