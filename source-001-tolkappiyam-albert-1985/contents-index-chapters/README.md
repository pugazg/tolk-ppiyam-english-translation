# Contents, Index and Chapters

This folder maintains navigation derived from the printed book structure while preserving the page-by-page transcription separately.

- [`contents.md`](contents.md) reproduces the printed contents and records the corresponding transcription locations.
- [`chapter-index.md`](chapter-index.md) records chapter boundaries as they are encountered and verified.

The printed source remains authoritative. Numbering, titles and page labels are not silently regularized. New chapters and index sections must be added here when their opening pages are reached.
