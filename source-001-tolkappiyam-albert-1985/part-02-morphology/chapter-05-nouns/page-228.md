# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Nine others

### 165

The following nine also belong to these: nouns `| palla |`, `| pala |`, `| cila |`, the nouns `| uḷḷa |`, `| illa |`, nouns where the verb governs its formation, nouns where the adjective governs its formation, cardinal numerals and the noun that makes the comparison.

## Generic name

### 166

Those generic names that take `| kaḷ |` belong to that plural form.

## Other names

### 167

Such other names in singular and plural showing the gender of the nouns belong to this class.

## By the verbs.

### 168

The non-personal generic name with the zero morph shows its singular plural number by the verb that follows.

## ——

### 169

All the nouns which are both personal and non-personal when they make this difference will not mark the gender except by the proper verb.

## ——

### 170

The present tense form which does not show the gender may show the personal class singular with the proper predicate.

## The common class

### 171

Proper names, names made after significant parts of the body⁴⁷, names where the latter is followed by the name of the person⁴⁸, names of relationship `| tām |`, `| tāṉ |`, `| ellām |`, `| nīyir |`, `| nī |` and such others that are not included here belong to the same.
