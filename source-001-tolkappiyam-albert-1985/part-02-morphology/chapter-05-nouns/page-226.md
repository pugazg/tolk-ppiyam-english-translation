# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Another fifteen — 160

The following fifteen are of the same nature. The word `| makaṉ |` which belongs to the masculine, the word `| makaḷ |` which belongs to the feminine, words with `| i |` ending which belongs to the feminine³¹ the words with `| i |` and `| ai |` following `| nam |`³² `| makaṉ |` and `| makaḷ |` when they do not indicate these relationships, the nouns `| māntar |` and `| makkaḷ |` the two words `| āṭūu |` and `| makaṭūu |`, `| aṉ |` and `| āṉ |` with the demonstratives at the initial position,³³ the word `| peṇṭu |` with these at the initial position and the word expressing likeness³⁴.

## Another three — 161

Scholars tell us that the noun `| ellārum |`, the noun `| ellīrum |` the noun `| makaṉ |` that goes with the feminine³⁵ are like the previous ones.

## Ten others — 162

Names of persons formed after the land,³⁶ the family³⁷ and the group³⁸, after one’s profession,³⁹ possession⁴⁰ and one’s nature⁴¹, nouns indicating more than one in the family relationship⁴² and in the significant part of the body⁴³, professional names in performances⁴⁴ and names showing the number of persons in a group⁴⁵—all these are of the same nature.

## With five gendres — 163

Such nouns showing plural and singular in the personal class with the different gender divisions belong to this class.

## Non-personal class — 164

These fifteen nouns `| atu |`, `| itu |`, `| utu |`, those with the `| āytam |` in this series, `| avai |`, `| ivai |`, `| uvai |`, those with the consonant `| v |` in this series⁴⁶ and `| yātu |`, `| yā |` and `| yāvai |` belong to the genders of the non personal class.
