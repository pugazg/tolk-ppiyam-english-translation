# 5. A Chapter on the Nouns

## Function of words — 152

All word. convey meaning.

## Meaning and form — 153

Scholars tell us that words reveal the object that is indicated and their grammatical form.

## Meaning — 154

It is said that meaning is indicated directly and by suggestion.

## Nouns and verbs — 155

Scholars tell us that words are of two kinds viz., nouns and verbs.

## Morphemes — 156

It is said that bound morphemes²⁹ and free morphemes³⁰ appear depending on these.

## Noun class — 157

Of these, the noun is found to be of three classes on examination viz., personal class, non-personal class and common class.

## Gender — 158

Nouns are classified into five gender divisions in the two classes.

## Fifteen personal class nouns — 159

In this manner, names that occur as `| avaṉ |`, `| ivaṉ |`; `| uvaṉ |`, `| avaḷ |`, `| ivaḷ |`, `| uvaḷ |`; `| avar |`, `| ivar |`, `| uvar |`; `| yāṉ |`, `| yām |`, `| nām |` and `| yāvaṉ |`, `| yāvaḷ |`, `| yāvar |` are the fifteen which show the gender in the personal class.
