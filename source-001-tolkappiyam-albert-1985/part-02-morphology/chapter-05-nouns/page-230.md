# Tolkāppiyam in English

## 172

It is said that among these there are four kinds of proper names, four kinds of names after the significant parts of body, four kinds where the latter is followed by the name of the persons, two kinds in the names of relationship and the rest in their own meaning.

## Proper names — 173

Among them, the four proper names are the feminine proper, the masculine proper, the plural proper and the singular proper.

## After parts of the body — 174

The four names after the significant parts of the body are the feminine, the masculine, the plural and the singular.

## + names of the persons — 175

The four names where the names of the significant parts of the body are followed by the name of the person are the one that shows the femine the one that shows the masculine the one that shows the plural and the one that shows the singular.

## Relationship names — 176

Names of masculine relationship and names of feminine relationship are the two relationship names.

## Feminine names — 177

All the nouns which show the feminine may go with the singular non-personal or the singular personal feminine.

## Masculine names — 178

All the nouns which show the masculine may go with the singular non-personal or the singular personal masculine.
