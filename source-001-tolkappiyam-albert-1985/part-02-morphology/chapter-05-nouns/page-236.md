## 51

The land is divided into five regions and in poetry the grand design of the use of the primary sources of life viz., Time and Space, the Nucleus of Life viz., god, tood, animal, tree bird, drum, profession and the lute and the five postures of love life in proper combination is insisted upon. Here the Nucleus of Life means these eight things that are found in each region as the central source of life’s activity. They go as non-personal class names of the region. This area is discussed in part III of Tolkāppiyam.

## 52

For example the word lion which is non-personal may be associated with a person of that stature of the king of animals. This verse says words are exempted from the rule of non-personal class names as pointed out in verse 196.
