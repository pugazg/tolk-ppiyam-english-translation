# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Strength — 360

`| vaya |` means strength.

## Bright — 361

`| vāḷ |` means brightness.

## Confusion — 362

`| tuyuvu |` means confusion in the mind.

## Suffering — 363

`| uyā |` means sufferings.

## Plot — 364

`| ucā |` means plotting in secrecy.

## Desire — 365

`| vayā |` means ardent desire.

## Anger — 366

`| kaṟuppu |` and `| civappu |` mean anger.

## Colour — 367

It is said that they mean colour also.

## Minuteness — 368

`| nocivu |`, `| nuḻaivu |` and `| nuṇaṅku |` mean minuteness.

## Time — 369

The morpheme `| puṉiṟu |` gives the sense of the time immediately after giving birth to.

## Extensive — 370

`| naṉavu |` means arena and extensiveness.

## Youth, Strength — 371

`| matavu |` means youth and strength.
