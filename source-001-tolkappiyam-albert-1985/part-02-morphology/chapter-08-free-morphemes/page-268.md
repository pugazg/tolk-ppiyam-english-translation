# 8. A Chapter on the Free Morphemes

## A description — 292

Description of a free morpheme: It takes its origin from sound, appelation and attributes. As noun and as verb it gives different meanings. Though one word claims a number of meanings and a number of words claim one meaning, if a more frequently used word is added to it in the place of a less frequently used one in the accepted way then every word will show its specific meaning.

## A list — 293

Since there is no need for the enumeration of the morphemes that can be clearly identified, free morphemes that are not clear are brought to our notice.

## Plenty — 294

They are three `| uṟu |`, `| tava |`, `| naṉi |` which give a sense of plenty.

## Fear, greatness — 295

`| uru |` means fear and `| purai |` means greatness.

## Colour — 296

`| kuru |` and `| keḻu |` mean colour.

## Distress — 297

`| cellal |` and `| iṉṉal |` mean distress.

## Fertility, abundance — 298

`| mallal |` means fertility and `| ē |` means abundance.
