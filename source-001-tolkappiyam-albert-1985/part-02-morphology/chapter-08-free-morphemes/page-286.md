how far Semantics is the study of meaningful units  
Chomsky insisted on the syntactical unit and other generative grammarians insisted on Semantics. Various other concepts like grammatical violation, selective restriction, referential obstruction have been developed. For a lucid expositton of this study one may refer to D. J. Allerton, Essentials of Grammatical Theory, A Consensus View of Syntax and Morphology (London : Routledge & Kegan Paul, 1979).

The following questions on Tolkāppiyam should be brought into this discussion.

i. Why does the Grammarian’s description of the free morphemes speak only of nouns and verbs as units of meaning ?

ii. Does he accomodate the use of allomorphs?

iii. Why does he find it necessary to say that some free morphs are also modifiers in verse 453 ?

iv. In enumerating the free morphemes that are not clear for identification indicating this in verse 296 does he make a provision for the infinite number of possibilities in language making the concept explicit?

v. In verse 295 when he describes the free morpheme does he not talk of the origin of meaning referring so usefully to sound, appelation and attributes ?

Thus a whole theory on whether the stronger principle is semantics or structure or how a balance is brought is to be formulated and it will be interesting to see which one of the schools among the generative grammarians Tolkāppiyam seems to come near to-
