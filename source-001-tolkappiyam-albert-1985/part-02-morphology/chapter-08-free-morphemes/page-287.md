Without entering into such detailed discussions, it is sufficient to say in this translation that we use free morpheme for `| uriccol |` and bound morpheme for `| iṭaiccol |` because as it was said, we do not like to balance a phrase from the area of semantics with a word from structural study, semantemes against morphemes. We prefer to have both the words from one area viz., structural study and it is sufficient to remember that morpheme is the concept that unites the area of meaning and structure.
