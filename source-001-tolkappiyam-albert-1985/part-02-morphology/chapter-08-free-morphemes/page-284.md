# Tolkāppiyam in English

## The method of substitution

### 383

All the free morphemes whose meanings have been indicated should be understood by examining what comes following and preceding them and thus the appropriate meaning should be taken. Then the meaning would appear in the proper perspective.

## Other meanings

### 384

The meanings so far given are not restrictive. If there are other meanings we must take them.

## Meaning of the meaning

### 385

There will be no limit if one tries to know the meaning of the meaning.

## The speaker’s sense

### 386

The meaning will not get confused if the listener gets the sense of the speaker.

## Feelings

### 387

The meaning depends on the strength of the feelings conveyed.

## Origin

### 388

The origin of the meaning of a word is never certain.

## Semantic units

### 389

It is not in the nature of this morpheme to be divided into meaningful units.

## Rules

### 390

Scholars tell us that since it is not possible to draw a line of limitation to the semantics of these and other free morphemes which are extensive in the various ways and since it is not what we aim at either, one must come to understand them clearly by following the right method according to what is said in the body of rules which are the safeguards.
