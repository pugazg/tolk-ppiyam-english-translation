# 4. Conventions Of the Vocative

## The Vocative — 115

We can say that what is known as the vocative goes with the particular noun distinctly.

## 116

What they are is laid down here for our understanding.

## Four endings — 117

Among them the names that take the vocative in the personal class are the four that take the final `| i |`, `| u |`, `| ai |` and `| ō |`.

## Mutation — 118

Of these `| i |` may become `| ī |` and `| ai |` may become `| āy |`.

## With `| ē |` — 119

`| ō |` and `| u |` take `| ē |`.

## Shorter `| u |` — 120

This `| u |` is the shorter `| u |`.

## Other than the four — 121

Scholars tell us that the other vowels in personal class nouns will not take the vocative.

## `| ī | + | i |` — 122

The elongated `| i |` at the end of a word remains without change in the vocative.

## `| ai | — | ā |` — 123

In the names of relations the `| ai |` ending may have to change to `| ā |`.
