# Conventions Of the Vocative — continued

## `| āḷ |` → `| āy |` — 143

The `| āḷ |` at the final position in nouns where the verb or the adjective governs their formation, becomes `| āy |` in the vocative.

## `| ḷ |` + relationship — 144

Such nouns expressing relationship²⁵ behave like the other nouns²⁶ of relationship.

## `| ḷ |` + demonstratives and interrogatives — 145

Scholars tell us that such demonstrative pronouns and interrogative pronouns behave as described earlier.

## Elongation — 146

Such²⁸ elongated nouns behave like the other elongated ones.

## Non-personal class — 147

The common nouns of the non-personal class with the endings described above take the vocative in the same manner.

## `+ | ē |` — 148

All the non-personal class nouns with consonants and vowels at the final place, take `| ē |` when they take the vocative.

## Objects at a distance — 149

All the nouns which are said to take the vocative will be further lengthened when used for an object at a distance.

## Empty morph — 150

Though the empty morph `| ammā |` with a final elongation does not show the noun of relation, scholars take it to mean a vocative.

## Non vocative — 151

Words indicating one's possession with `| t |`, `| n |`, `| nu |`, `| e |` at the initial position and `| ṉ |`, `| r |` and `| ḷ |` at the final position and such nouns do not take the vocative.
