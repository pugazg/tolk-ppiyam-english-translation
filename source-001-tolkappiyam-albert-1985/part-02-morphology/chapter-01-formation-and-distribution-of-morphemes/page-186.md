## ———

### 34

The same is done when an imaginary thing is mentioned.

## Formation of the structures of answers

### 35

When a certain thing is asked for and if it is not available, it is right to say in answer that something else is available.⁶

## Use of the demonstrative

### 36

When this object is mentioned, the demonstrative should be used.

## None-use——

### 37

Even if the proper demonstrative is not used along with the object the sense will be the same and not different.

## Placement of proper nouns

### 38

Scholars tell us that when a proper noun and a demonstrative pronoun are catenated with verbs the demonstrative pronoun will not precede but will follow the proper noun.

## In versification

### 39

In versification it may precede.

## The place for therefore

### 40

The word, with the demonstrative initial meaning cause is placed the way demonstrative pronoun is placed.

## The place for titles

### 41

The proper name should not precede the title of distinction.
