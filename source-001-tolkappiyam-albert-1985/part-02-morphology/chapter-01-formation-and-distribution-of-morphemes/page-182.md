## Deviations

### 17

It is not forbidden to use deviations that are accepted because of usage and propriety.

## Redundancy

### 18

The use of an adjective whose antonym cannot qualify the noun, is not customary except in versification.

## Description of natural things

### 19

The elements are described with set phrases, showing their original nature.

## —artificial—

### 20

Things that have been shaped are described with the word meaning ‘to become’.

## The position of ‘to become’

### 21

The indication of the agents of change should precede the word meaning ‘to become’.

## Without an agent

### 22

It is not a wrong usage if the word ‘to become’ occurs without the agent.

## Doubtful gender

### 23

If the gender is in doubt the plural of the class is used.⁵

### 24

To point out the proper gender ending for the two classes of words, the word `| uru |` or the name itself is used.

## Syntactical value in preference to placement value

### 25

The word that exposes the doubt agrees in gender with the word to which it is syntactically concorded though the word in proximity is of a different gender.
