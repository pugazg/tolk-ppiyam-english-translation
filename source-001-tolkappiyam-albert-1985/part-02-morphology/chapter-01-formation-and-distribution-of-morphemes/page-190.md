## Common gender names

### 50

Those nouns and predicates which take the common gender forms need not be confused if the common traditional usage is taken into consideration.

## Ending in verse

### 51

For a series of personal class and non-personal class nouns in versification the ending may be of the non-personal class mostly.

## Homonyms

### 52

There are two classes of homonyms. Some take different predicates; some take the same.

## —— + different verb

### 53

Of these the meaning of the homonyms that take different verbs is known by the use of these verbs and the words that go with them in the context.

## —— + same verb

### 54

If the same verb is used then distinctions must be made with the help of proper adjuncts.

### 55

When the noun and the verb are both homonyms the distinction must be made out explicitly.
