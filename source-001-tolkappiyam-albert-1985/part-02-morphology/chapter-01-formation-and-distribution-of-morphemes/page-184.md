## Order in a noun phrase

### 26

The three, the adjective, the part and the object as the head word are placed in this right order as the adjective shows the colour.

## Plural for respect

### 27

The use of plural with a single person or a single thing to indicate respect is in usage and this is not governed by grammar rules.

## Four of the words

### 28

Forms derived from the four words `| celavu |`, `| varavu |`, `| taravu |` and `| koṭai |` can be assigned to usages in the three places, the first, the second and the third person.

## Two of them

### 29

Among them the two morphemes `| taru |` and `| varu |` go with the two places of the first person and the third person.

## The other——

### 30

The other two are used in the other place.

## Interrogative

### 31

The two morphemes `| yātu |` and `| evaṉ |` are used to make questions about meanings that are not known.

## `| yātu |`

### 32

Of these the interrogative morpheme `| yātu |` is also used for asking further questions to clear doubts about things that are already known.

## `| um |`

### 33

When the number of the parts of a whole is mentioned in a phrase, `| um |` is used as the verb is introduced in the structure.
