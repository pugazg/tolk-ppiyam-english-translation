# Notes

## 1

This chapter seems to discuss the formation, co-ordination and placement of morphemes. These three naturally show the formation of phrases and sentences in their syntactical form. The Tamil word used by the Grammarian corresponds neatly to the English phrase formation and distribution. This Tamil word shows the formations of the morpheme and the formations it makes in larger entities like words, phrases and sentences. Happily the English phrase used here stands for formation co-ordination and placement, the three processes described in this chapter.

## 2

The Tamil word `| pāl |` means division and it is rendered here as gender. The word gender in English also means division since the original Latin and French word meant a kind. Dictionaries say gender means distinction of words roughly answering to sex. This semantic change of Restriction of the word `| pāl |` though applicable in Tamil usage is not applicable in the case of grammar categories. In Tamil `| pāl |` rendered here as gender is a category label that describes a sex-number-person division. In Old Literary Tamil there were five gender divisions. The masculine, feminine and the Epicine plural in the personal class names and the singular plural in the non-personal class names. For a fuller discussion see Pon Kothandaraman, “On Gender systems,” _Linguistic Studies in Tamil_ (Madras: Tamil Nuulagam, 1980) and S. V. Shunmugam, _Dravidian Nouns : A Comparative Study_ (Annamalai Nagar : Annamalai University, 1971).
