## Non-Personal singular ending

### 8

Words which show the non-personal singular, and with the shorter `| u |` that goes with `| t |`, `| ṟ |` and `| ṭ |`.

## Non-Personal plural

### 9

The three `| a |`, `| ā |` and `| va |` give the ending to the words showing the many.

## Verbal endings

### 10

The eleven endings themselves showing the five genders in the two classes appear with the verbs.

## Agreement

### 11

Action words showing the gender and naming words showing the gender should not be confused in agreement.

## Womanish

### 12

Naming words which show that the maleness is dormant will not take the masculine ending.

## Questions and answers

### 13

Answers and questions must pair suitably.

## Question as answer

### 14

When a question is shot against it becomes an answer.

## In different forms

### 15

It cannot be objected to if the answer is in a different structure from the question, provided the words show a recognition of the question.³

## Co-ordination

### 16

In answers and questions the head words and the modifiers should co-ordinate.⁴
