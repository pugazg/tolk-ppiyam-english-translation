# 1. Formation and Distribution of Morphemes¹

## The two classes

### 1

Those which denote human beings are personal class and the rest are non-personal. Words are used in these two classes.

## Three Genders

### 2

Words showing the male, words showing the female and words showing the many are the three genders² of the personal class words.

## The two genders

### 3

Words showing the one and words showing the many are the two genders of the non-personal class words.

## Two of the personal class categories

### 4

Naming words which denote the personal class female where the maleness is dormant and also names of gods do not have specific endings but they take the different genders of the personal class.

## Masculine ending

### 5

The consonant | ṉ | shows the masculine.

## Feminine ending

### 6

The consonant | ḷ | shows the feminine.

## Personal Class plural ending

### 7

The consonant | r |, the final | pa | and | mār |, these three appear to show the personal class plural words.
