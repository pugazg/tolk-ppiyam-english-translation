## A caution

### 56

One has to use the right expression to convey one’s idea.

## Abstract names

### 57

These eighteen nouns showing status, manliness, youth, old age, slavery, strength, hospitality, group, womanhood, Kingship, boyhood, childhood, metaphors, names of parts, words of endearment, honour, hatred, valour, similar ones and words that are understood only in an abstraction take the non personal class predicates though these qualities belong to the personal class.

## Personal class without its gender division

### 58

These ten words showing time, the world, the soul, the body, the god of our destiny, action, the elements, the sun, the moon and such words will not show the gender distinction of the personal class.

## In their forms

### 59

It is not in their natural form these show this distinction.

## In different forms

### 60

They may do so if they change their forms.

## Semantic area

### 61

An utterance may be used to express related ideas.

## Number and forms

### 62

Words showing parts of the body in plural like the eye, shoulder, breast and such words need not take the plural form unless their verbs have the same.
