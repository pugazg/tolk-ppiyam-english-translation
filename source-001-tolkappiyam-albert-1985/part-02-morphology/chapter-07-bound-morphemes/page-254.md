# 7. A Chapter on the Bound Morphemes

## A description — 244

Bound morphemes occur along with nouns and verbs; they do not occur independently.

## A list — 245

On examination they show the following functions. They appear in between words giving meaning as they join, appear with verbs when they show time, appear as markers in showing the casee, as empty m,rphs, as euphonic fillers, as onomatopoeic words and as the morphemes that give the sense in comparisons.

## Its position — 246

It is said that it may appear in all these ways following or preceding words, deforming its endings and taking with it another of its kind.

## `| maṉ |`

### 247

The morpheme `| maṉ |` appears to express nostolgia for what is lost, wonder at a complete change and irony at what is implied.

## `| til |`

### 248

The morphsme `| til |` appears to introduce the three expressions of a great desire, of time and implied meaning.

## `| koṉ |`

### 249

The morpheme `| koṉ |` appears to introduce the four implied expressions of fea, uselessness, time and greatness.
