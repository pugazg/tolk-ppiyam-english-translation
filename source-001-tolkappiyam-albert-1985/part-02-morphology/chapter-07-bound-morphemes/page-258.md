# Tolkāppiyam in English

## `| eṟṟu |` — 258

`| eṟṟu |` occurs to express the sense of loss.

## `| maṟṟaiyatu |` — 259

The morpheme `| maṟṟaiyatu |` points out to the one that is related to and not what is mentioned.

## `| maṉṟa |` — 260

The morpheme `| maṉṟa |` specifies the clarity of choice.

## `| tañcam |` — 261

The morpheme `| tañcam |` specifies easiness.

## `| antil |` — 262

`| antil |` stands for place and as a euphonic empty morph.

## `| kol |` — 263

`| kol |` expresses doubt.

## `| el |` — 264

`| el |` expresses luminosity.

## `| ār |` — 265

The morpheme `| ār |` following a generic name requires a personal class plural ending in the predicate.

## ——

### 266

It can also occur as a euphonic empty morph.

## `| ē |` and `| kuṟai |` — 267

`| ē |` and `| kuṟai |` satisfy the two needs of extra phonemes and extra syllables.

## `| mā |` — 268

The morpheme `| mā |` is the empty morph in expressions of wish and praise.
