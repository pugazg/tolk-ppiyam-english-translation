# Tolkāppiyam in English

## `| ē |` and `| ō |` — 277

`| ē |` at the final position in `| naṉṟu |`, `| ē |` at the final position in `| aṉṟu |`, `| ō |` at the final position in `| antu |` and `| ō |` at the final position in `| aṉ |` and similar ones convey their meaning by the tone expressed.

## `| um |` — 278

`| um |` added to a premodifier and `| um |` used along with a negative cannot be placed together.

## ______ placement — 279

If it is not actually used with the words where they should, these words are placed first and not after.

## ______ exclusiveness — 280

The `| um |` which shows inclusiveness as it occurs with numerals may even indicate exclusiveness.

## One measure `| ē |` — 281

The `| ē |` that occurs at the final position may in utterances rightly become one measure.

## `| um |` and `| eṉavē |` — 282

The `| um |` in enumeration and `| eṉavē |` used in enumeration do not necessarily occur with the numerals after them.

## `| ē |` — 283

Though the `| ē |` used in enumeration appears only at intervals, scholars tell us that this is sufficient for enumeration.

## `| eṉā |` and `| eṉṟā |` — 284

The two morphemes `| eṉā |` and `| eṉṟā |` with a final `| ā |` are used in enumeration in the place of `| um |`.
