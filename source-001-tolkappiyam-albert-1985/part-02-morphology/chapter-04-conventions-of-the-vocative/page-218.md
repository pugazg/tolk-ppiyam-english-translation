# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Other `| ṉ |` endings.

### 134

The noun `| tāṉ |`, the demonstrative pronoun, the noun `| yāṉ |` and the interrogative pronoun will not take the vocative.

## `| r |` ending

### 135

`| ār |` and `| ar |` will change to `| īr |`.

## —— gerund

### 136

If this is a gerund the change to `| ē |` is not forbidden by the scholars.

## Nouns with adjectival stems ____

### 137

In such nouns¹⁹ where the adjective governs the formation of the noun, the same holds good.

## Elongation

### 138

The elongated one²⁰ behaves like the other elongated one²¹.

## Demonstratives with `| r |` ending

### 139

The demonstrative pronoun behaves as described earlier²².

## Two others

### 140

The two, the deformed morph of `| num |` and the interrogative noun behave as shown earlier²³.

## The other two

### 141

In the remaining two endings²⁴ the last but one gets elongated.

## —— long sounds

### 142

If the last but one is a long sound, there will be no change.
