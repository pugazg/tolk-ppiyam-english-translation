# 3. A Chapter On The Case-Marker Substitution

## Two and seven — 80

When leaning is not used in its literal sense, the case marker `| kaṇ |` can also be used.

## 81

Scholars tell us that when nouns naming the parts go with verbs `| ai |` and `| kaṇ |` are interchangeable.

## 82

The words `| kaṉṟal |` and `| celavu |` take the same when they form the verbs.

## Six followed by two — 83

If the word naming the part follows that of the whole taking `| atu |`, the verb takes `| ai |`.

## Two followed by seven — 84

Scholars tell us that if the word naming the whole comes first with `| ai |`, it is clear that the case marker `| kaṇ |` follows the word that names the part.

## The part and the whole — 85

The way words are employed should indicate the difference between the words naming the parts and the whole.

## Heaps — 86

The names of heaps of things do not deviate from this rule. This usage is being followed from the ancient times.
