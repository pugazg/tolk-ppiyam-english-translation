# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Four and six — 95

The word showing a receiver where `| ku |` is implied will mean the same with the sixth.

## Five and two — 96

With the word expressing fear, the fifth and the second are used rightly in the expanded form.

## General — 97

In all these cases of case marker alternatives and meanings, and in such similar cases where the ancient rules are faithfully followed those who can see the alternative case markers clearly will have no confusion.

## In a series — 98

In a series where the words are followed by case markers the meaning will be complete with one word at the end.

## Placement — 99

It is not forbidden to place the case marker either at the end or in the middle provided the right emphasis is brought out.¹³

## Ancient rules — 100

It is said that the practice of interchanging and shedding case markers is in accordance with the ancient rules.

## Two and seven — 101

Except `| ai |` and `| kaṇ |` the other case markers will not stand implied at the end of utterances.

## Marker’s value — 102

Whatever might be the case marker its implication is to go according to the meaning of the utterance.
