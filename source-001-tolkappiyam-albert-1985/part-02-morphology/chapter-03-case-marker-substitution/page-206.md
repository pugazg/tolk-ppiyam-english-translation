# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Three

### 87

`| otu |` goes with the noun showing the superior one when a single verb follows them.

## Three and five

### 88

Scholars tell us that the third and the fifth case markers do the same job as the word `| ākkam |` goes with the word that shows the cause of that making.

## ——

### 89

The word `| nōkkal |` used in the figurative sense with the second case marker may also be used with these two.

## Six and four

### 90

The case marker `| atu |`, when it follows a personal class noun, may disappear giving place to `| ku |`.

## Two and three

### 91

When the voice of the verb is uncertain it is not wrong to use the second and the third case markers to make the meaning clear.

## ——

### 92

The difference can be understood by what follows the last naming word which gives the utterance the right meaning.

## ——

### 93

In a construction with the imperative ‘save’ `| ai |` and `| āṉ |` may be equally used when the marker is implied.¹²

## Six and seven

### 94

The seventh case marker may appear to show the place of habitation where the sixth appeared to show this living condition.
