# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Usage in negations

### 103

Even in negative utterances the case markers will no change the nature of their usage.

## Four, two, three

### 104

In versification the final `| ku |`, `| ai |`, and `| āṉ |` may take `| a |`.

## In non-personal class

### 105

It is said that in non-personal class names `| ku |` and `| ai |` will not take `| a |`.

## Four

### 106

In accordance with the ancient usage the fourth case marker can be used with the name of the whole followed by that of the part in the utterance where the nature of the part is expressed, with the name of the medium with which something is exchanged, with the name of the thing by which something is considered fit to be done, with the name of the person whose relation is expressed, with the word that marks the boundary, with the word that makes a comparison, with the name of a thing about which dislike is expressed, with the name of a thing for which one gives up one’s inclination and with noun expressing finality.

## Other markers

### 107

It is not wrong to use the other case markers in these ways in utterances.

## Constituents

### 108

It is said that the eight primary constituent units of any utterance are action, agent, object, place, time and instrument along with, the receiver and the result in the sense of ‘for whom’ and ‘for what’.

## In usage

### 109

Of these some may not be employed in common usage.
