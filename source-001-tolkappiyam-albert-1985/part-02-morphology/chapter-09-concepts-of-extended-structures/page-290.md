# 9. A Chapter on the Concepts of Extended Structures

## Four kinds of words — 391

Primitives, derivatives, dialectical and the northern words are the words used in poetry.

## Primitives — 392

Among them the primitives are those that are used without change of meaning in conformity with the usage obtained in the Tamil country.

## Derivatives — 393

Derivative words are of two kinds, the many words that give one sense and the one word that denotes many.

## Dialectical — 394

Dialectical words are those that are used in the twelve territories of the Tamil land with regional variations.

## Northern Words — 395

Northern words are those words which shed their scripts and are adapted.

## Deformed ones — 396

Even when they get deformed they are not excluded if they become suitable in the new environment.

## Phonemic Metanalysis — 397

When these four kinds of words are used in utterances, it becomes necessary that those that need to be made hard should be made hard, those that need to be made soft should be made soft, those that are to be added are to be added, those that are to be elided are to be elided, those to be lengthened are to be lengthened and those to be shortened are to be shortened.
