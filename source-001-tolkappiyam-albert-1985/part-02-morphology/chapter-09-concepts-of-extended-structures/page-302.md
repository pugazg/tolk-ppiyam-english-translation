# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Give =

### 438

The three morphemes `| ī |`, `| tā |`, `| koṭu |` occur as expressive of asking.

## `| ī |` =

### 439

Among them the morpheme `| ī |` is used by the inferior person.

## `| tā |` =

### 440

The morpheme `| tā |` is used by an equal.

## `| koṭu |`

### 441

The morpheme `| koṭu |` is used by the superior person.

## Change of person

### 442

Scholars tell us that though the morpheme `| koṭu |` belongs to the third person, if it is in the context of calling one’s self as another person it may belong to the first person.

## Application of rules

### 443

Naming words that are interchangeable⁸², dialectical words that are interchangeable, interchangeable words established by long usage, interchangeable semantic misunderstandings, such words in incantatory formulas do not go by prescribed rules.

## `| ceyyāy |`

### 444

There are places where the seccnd person verb `| ceyyāy |` becomes the word `| cey |`.

## `| ī |` and `| ē |`

### 445

`| ī |` and `| ē |` following thc second person verbs take the appropriate consonants.
