# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Compounds — 406

Case compounds, compounds of comparisons, compounds of verbs, adjectival compounds, conjunctional compounds and zero word combounds are the six occurrences where there are word gaps.⁸¹

## Case — 407

Case compounds are of the case markers.

## Comparison — 408

Compounds of comparisons are of the comparisons.

## Verb — 409

Verb compounds are of the time indicated.

## Adjective — 410

Words expressing the qualities of colour, shape, measure and taste and the like taking the words of the object of these qualities immediately after are the adjectival compounds.

## Conjunction — 411

Conjunctional compounds occur with the following six words: two or more names, names of measures, names indicated by numbers, names of measures and names of numerals.

## Zero — 412

The zero word compound occurs at the final places in words of adjectival compounds, conjunctional compounds and case compounds.

## Semantic value — 413

Among these the semantic value may concentrate in four ways. It may fall on the word that follows or on the preceding word or on both with equal force or it may not be on these but on the one which is not there.
