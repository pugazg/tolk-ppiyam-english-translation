# Tolkāppiyam in English

## ——

### 422

All the different types of verbs take these positions only.

## Strings of verbs

### 423

Of these, even as they become repetitive in different indications of meaning they end only with a name.

## Modifiers

### 424

Modifiers indicating semantic changes in the structures are of ten kinds: modifiers in the structure of exclusion, modifiers in the verbal phrase structure, modifiers in the noun phrase, modifiers suggesting what has not been said in the structure, modifiers suggesting their negation, the `| um |` modifier, the `| ena |` modifier, the modifier of indicative value, the modifier of euphonic value and the word modifier.

## Exclusion

### 425

Among them, the premodifier in the structure of exclusion ends with the word that expresses this sense of exclusion.

## Verbal phrase structure

### 426

In a verbal phrase structure the premodifier takes either a full verb ending or a defective verb ending. In this the defective occurs in the sense of being formed into something.

## The noun phrase Structure

### 427

The noun phrase Structure ends with a noun.

## Structures of Exclusion.

### 428

Structures suggesting what has not been said end with these endings.
