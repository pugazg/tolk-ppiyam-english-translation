## 81

The word compound used in this section has a technical meaning in as far as it carries the meaning of the Tamil word `| tokai |`. This word means things that come by addition (compounds) and at the same time an elision or gap. This concept is not peculiar to Tamil. Eye-ball is a compound; there is elision too in the ball of the eye or in a cruder form, Eye’s ball. There is a gap as the possessive is not marked. This leaves a gap there.
