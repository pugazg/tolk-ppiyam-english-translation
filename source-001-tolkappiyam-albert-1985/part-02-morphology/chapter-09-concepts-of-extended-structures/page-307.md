# Notes

## 80

It requires some explanation to show why the Tamil word `| eccam |` is translated here as concepts of extended structures. Many commentators simplify the problem and say that this word indicates that the Grammarian puts into this chapter whatever it was not convenient to put into the previous eight chapters. The word `| eccam |` of course means whatever is left over. But we must bear in mind that ancient Grammars delighted in quibbles, disquisitions and words with multiple meanings. This is not absent in Tolkāppiyam though in a translation we do not have many opportunities to point out these. This is perhaps the only occasion where the translation required the explanation of this aspect.

Take for example the morpheme `| kaṇṭīrē |` in verse 422. `| kaṇṭīr |` is a verb and so `| kaṇṭīrē |` cannot usefully go to that chapter; `| ē |` is a bound morpheme and so, again `| kaṇṭīrē |` cannot go to that chapter. But here is a concept of the extended structure. This idea is reinforced when we see how this chapter talks also on the meaning of extended structures in versification. It also talks about the modifiers in structures (v. 427 - v. 438) which again embodies the concept of extended structures in additions, in inter-changeability, in elsions.

Evidently these cannot go into the earlier chapters. Any explanation that tries to show that the Grammarian failed to put some concepts in the previous chapters and therefore he gathers them here in one last chapter is faulty. The second layer of meaning that this chapter merely gathers those usages which cannot be distinctly labelled under the previous headings of the chapter is not learned enough. Obviously this idea is there. But this chapter talks about structures in which morphemes that fell into other chapters have now extended structural forms.
