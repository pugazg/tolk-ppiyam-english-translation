# Tolkāppiyam in English

## 456

Ending a word in the second person singular with a plural marker is not forbidden and this is used in the context of some one being guided.

## Split and analysis — 457

All the words whose meaning is made clear in literature and in usage should be understood by proper division and analysis without infringing the rules of grammar.
