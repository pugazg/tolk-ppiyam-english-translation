# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Like a single word

### 414

All compounds behave as one word.

## Plural form

### 415

Scholars tell us that personal class conjunctional compounds take the plural form.

## Figurative speech

### 416

When something which does not move is said to be moving or saying that it speaks while it does not, and all such expressions only point out what actually happens by this reference.

## Euphonic repetition

### 417

The limit for euphonic repetition is four.

## Repetition for immediacy

### 418

The limit for repetition for expressions of immediacy is three.

## Fillers

### 419

It is said that the following `| kaṇṭīr |`, `| koṇṭīr |`, `| ceṉṟatu |` and `| pōyiṟṟu |` when they take the interrogative form become syllabic fillers.

## ——

### 420

The following `| kēṭṭai |`, `| niṉṟai |`, `| kāttai |`, `| kaṇṭai |` when they are not used with the second person become syllabic fillers as pointed out above.

## Verb placement

### 421

The two kinds of verbs, the full and the defective in their semantic form go with the six places of the three distinctly decipherable time units of the present, past and future and the three places of the first, second and third persons to form the finite verbs.
