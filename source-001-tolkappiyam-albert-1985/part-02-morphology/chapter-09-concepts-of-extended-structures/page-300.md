# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Negative suggestion. — 429

Structures suggesting their negatives end with these endings.

## `| um |` — 430

The modifier `| um |` appears in the structure at the end of the two words with the same verb.

## Tense with `| um |` — 431

When this is followed by a structure free from `| um |` it is not wrong to use the past along with the future and the present along with the future.

## `| ena |` — 432

The modifier `| ena |` takes a verb for the ending.

## The rest — 433

It is said that the remaining three do not have the word-modifier to complete them.

## By indication — 434

They show the semantic function of the modifier by what they indicate.

## Placement and form — 435

The word-modifier keeps itself either after or before as a word and not as anything beyond this form.

## Euphemism — 436

An utterance that cannot be said in a congregation should be conveyed in a concealed way.

## Deformed usages — 437

When speaking in the concealed form we should not avoid the traditional deformed usages.
