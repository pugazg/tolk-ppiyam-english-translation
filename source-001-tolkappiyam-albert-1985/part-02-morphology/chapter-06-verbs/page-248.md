# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Elision — 233

Of these, scholars tell us that in the modifier `| ceyyum |` of noun phrases, the `| u |` along with its consonant at the penultimate place disappears and that place in usage is to be assessed.

## Past and future — 234

`| ceytu |` , the premodifier of the past may go with the future.

## The universal present — 235

We must use the present tense as the common form when all the three times are expressed in any occurrence.

## Aspect of quick completion — 236

Scholars tell us that the verb that shows both the future and the present is used in its past form to show the aspect of hasty completion of action.

## The conditional with the present — 237

If the verb expresses conditions that bring very special results, when we indicate this result where the agent is not specified⁷⁴, the present tense will bring out the right meaning.

## Command or wish — 238

The word that expresses that this is to be done indicates the two persons, the direct and the indirect agents⁷⁵.

## Questions, Negatives — 239

The verb in the interrogative of insistence can also express the negative.

## Past, present form, the future — 240

When the action is natural and certain the past and the present can be used in the place of the verb in the future tense.
