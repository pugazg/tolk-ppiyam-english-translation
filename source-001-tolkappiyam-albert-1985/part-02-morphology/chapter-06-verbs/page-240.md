# Tolkāppiyam in English

## Third person singular — 202

The four `| aṉ |`, `| āṉ |`, `| aḷ |` and `| āḷ |` are third person singular morphemes.

## ——plural

### 203

The three `| ar |`, `| ār |`, and `| pa |` are the third person plural morphemes.

## `| mār |`

### 204

The word with the morpheme `| mār |` takes the personal class plural verbal ending.

## Twenty three endings — 205

These twenty three⁵⁴ already mentioned belong to the personal class in singular and plural gender forms.

## The defective — 206

Of these-the first personal plural may become defective when used in enumeration.

## `| yār |`

### 207

The word `| yār |` used for asking questions in that class⁵⁵ goes with the three genders⁵⁶.

## `| ā |` → `| ō |`

### 208

The `| ā |` in these three⁵⁷ endings that show the genders may change to `| ō |` in poetry.

## `| āy |` → `| ōy |`

### 209

The morpheme `| āy |` behaves in the same manner.

## Implied time — 210

With the case marker morpheme `| atu |` showing possessive names⁵⁸, with the case marker morpheme indicating names by place⁵⁹, with comparatives⁶⁰ and indications of quality⁶¹, time is always implied.

Time is only implied in formations with morphemes expressing negation,⁶² with morphemes expressing absence⁶³, morphemes expressing presence⁶⁴, those expressing capability⁶⁵ and in all such formations of implied time.
