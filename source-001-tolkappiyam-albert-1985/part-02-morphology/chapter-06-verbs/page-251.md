# Notes

## 53

The concept of implied time is dealt with by the Grammarian because throughout this grammar we find an effort to be always explicit as all Linguistics should do.

For examples of words where the time sense is only implied see notes numbered 58 through 65.

Verse 213 lists these morphemes in their semantic conditions.

## 58

The Tamil word `| poṉṉaṉ |` is the equivalent of the one who possesses gold. The time sense expressed explicitly in the English phrase is only implied in the Tamil word `| poṉṉaṉ |`.

## 59

The time element found in the phrase, one who occupies or possesses this estate is only implied in the equivalent Tamil word `| tōṭṭattāṉ |`

## 60

The Tamil phrase `| oppāṉ |` shows time only in an implied way and not explicitly as in the English rendering of `| oppāṉ |` He is like——.

## 61

`| kariyaṉ |` the Tamil word shows time but not explicitly as in the English rendering: He is dark.
