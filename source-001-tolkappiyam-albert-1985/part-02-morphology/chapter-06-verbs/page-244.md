## Singular endings — 218

Of these, the second person endings and the three `| i |`, `| ai |`, `| āy |` appear with the singular in both the classes.

## Plural endings — 219

Scholars tell us that the three endings `| ir |`, `| īr |` and `| miṉ |` appear with the plural in both the classes.

## The rest — 220

The rest of the morphemes⁷¹ will appear in the five genders in the personal places.

## Expressions of wish — 221

Among these the expression of wish may not often go with the second and first person.

## `| ceyyum |` — 222

The word `| ceyyum |` showing the present will not occur with the following three-the third person plural the second and the first person.

## Premodifiers of verbal phrases — 223

The nine premodifiers of verbal phrases are `| ceytu |`, `| ceyyū |`, `| ceypu |`, `| ceyta a |`, `| ceyyiyar |`, `| ceyyiya |`, `| ceyin |`, `| ceya |` and `| ceyaṟku |`.

## Other—— — 224

Words such as `| piṉ |`, `| muṉ |`, `| kāl |`, `| kaṭai |`, `| vaḻi |` and `| iṭattu |` which indicate time do the same function.
