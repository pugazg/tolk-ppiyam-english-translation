# Tolkāppiyam in English

## 3. Tolkāppiyam An Attempt at Explicitness

Explicitness as understood in Generative grammar seems to be at the basis of Tolkāppiyam. Note numbered 53 refers to this.

**Basic reading:** Frank Palmer, _Grammar_, 1971.

## 4. Concepts of Structure in Tolkāppiyam

Labels like premodifier of a verbal phrase `| viṉaiyeñcu |` `| kiḷavi |` or premodifier of a noun phrase `| peyreñcukiḷvai |` and others show the concept of structure was at the basis of the concepts in Tolkāppiyam. A study of this will remove the wide spread prejudice that Tamil Grammar lacks a structural approach.

**Basic reading:** M. Berry, _Introduction to Systemic Linguistics I; Structures and Systems_ (London: Batsford, 1975).

## 5. Tolkāppiyam : A Balance between Semantics and Structuralism.

One way of approach in language study is the analysis of meaning unit. Another is of the structure. The way Tolkāppiyam considers the two bases is an interesting study. This has been referred to in notes numbered 79.

**Basic reading** J. A. Allerton, _Essentials of Grammatical Theory. A Consensus View of Syntax and Morohology_,1979.

Considerations of the five studies suggested above will form the basis of this study.
