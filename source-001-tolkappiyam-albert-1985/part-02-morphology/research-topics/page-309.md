# 10. Research Topics

Here are six research topics on Tolkāppiyam Part II Morphology. Hints and suggestions are given here and they should be made use of for making hard and richly rewarding findings.

A basic reading is suggested for the topics.

## 1. A Grammar based on Distinctions of Morphology, Function and structure.

Noun or verb or adjective or adverb is of morphological categories. Subject or object or adjunct or complement is of the functional category. Nominal, verbal, adjectival or adverbial is of the placement category in a structure. One must realize the importance and implications of these distinctions.

Tolkāppiyam seems to be aware of such distinctions. One can see how the verb is defined in verse 198. The noun is the morpheme that takes the name. In verse 200 a distinction is made between time and tense the grammatical form and the time concept. These distinctions and their implications to the structuralists as well as the Generative Grammarians will form an interesting study.

**Basic Reading:** Christophersen, _Higher English Grammar_, 1952.

## 2. The Methodology of Survey, Sampling and Enumeration in Tolkāppiyam.

After the definitions we find in almost all the chapters surveys samples and an enumeration of the particular categories This method belongs to a definite approach in grammar theory.

**Basic reading:** Philip W. Davis _Modern Theories of Language_, 1973.
