## Suffixation

### 70

It is said that these case markers listed above are suffixed to the nouns without any change in their value.

## Nouns with verbal stems

### 71

Words which denote names do not show time except when they are formed suffixed to verbs.

## Case two

### 72

The case marker called `| ai |` is the second and scholars tell us that wherever it occurs it is followed with a finite verb or an infinite and these are protecting, driving away, riding, building up, comparing, praising, despising, acquiring, losing, loving, getting angry, conquering, rejoicing, learning, cutting, shortening, collecting, dividing, weighing, measuring, counting, manufacturing, depending, going, hating, looking at, fearing, shattering and others and such words expressing these actions⁹.

## Case three

### 73

The case marker called `| oṭu |` is the third. It goes with the agentive and the instrumental. Scholars tell us that it goes with the word that denotes the material cause followed by the predication. It may occur giving the sense of becoming something, consisting of, mixing with, in the sense of along with either showing a single action or two actions, showing a comparison with contrastive features, in pointing out the nature of persons or the cause of things and with other such words.

## Case four

### 74

The case marker called `| ku |` is the fourth. It always shows the idea of receiving whatever be the actual word meaning¹⁰. It comes to stand for the phrase ‘for which’ in an action, ‘to which’ in an agreement
