## Case five

### 75

The case marker called `| iṉ |` is the fifth. It shows: This is the relation to this. Scholars tell us that they express colour, shape, measure, taste, coolness, hotness, fear, goodness, evil, smallness, largeness, hardness, softness, ferocity, agedness, youth, superiority, inferiority, newness, oldness, resource, absence, presence, smell, separateness, multitudiness, fewness, detachment and such other qualities.

## Case six

### 76

The case marker called `| atu |` is the sixth. It expresses subjective and objective relations. Scholars tell us that this shows relations with the words nature, possession, relationship, kinship, action, maturity, occupation, instrument,association, document; capital, parts of the unit, group, composition, state, residence and such categories of relations.

## Case seven

### 77

The seventh case marker is the one known by the name `| kaṇ |`. It occurs to show the place and time in an action. Scholars tell us that `| kaṇ |`, `| kāl |`, `| puṟam |`, `| akam |`, `| uḷ |`, `| uḻai |`, `| kīḻ |`, `| mēl |`, `| piṉ |`, `| cār |`, `| ayal |`, `| puṭai |`, `| tēvakai |`, `| muṉ |`, `| iṭai |`, `| kaṭai |`, `| talai |`, `| valam |`, `| iṭam |` and such words belong to this.

## Case gap

### 78

When the case gap is to be filled in for expansion it should be done as a suffix to the noun.

## Alternatives

### 79

All the words showing the various meanings of these case markers can be legitimately used.
